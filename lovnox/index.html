Who we are

A small studio with an outsized obsession for craft.

Lovnox started as a two-person side project and grew into a compact, senior-only studio. We work with founders, product teams and cultural brands who care about how things look, feel and perform — not just that they exist.

Every engagement begins with the same question: what is the single idea this brand must own? From there we build a system — type, colour, motion, voice, code — that scales from a favicon to a billboard without losing its soul.

  • Strategy-first design — no decoration without a reason.
  • Design and engineering in the same room, from day one.
  • Accessible, fast, and measurable — beauty that loads in under a second.
